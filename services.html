More Tailoring, Embroidery & Finishing Services

Our service range covers custom ladies tailoring, blouse work, garment alterations, computer embroidery, Maggam and embellishment work, saree finishing, kids wear and uniform/logo embroidery. Specific designs and work types are taken based on the garment and order requirement.

👗 Ladies & Blouse

Ladies dress stitching, custom measurements, pattern cutting, fitting alterations, sleeve/neck/waist/length adjustments, simple and designer blouses, embroidery blouses, zari-style work, neckline and sleeve detailing, blouse resizing and finishing.

💻 Computer Embroidery

Custom computer embroidery, names, initials, motifs, borders, patches, appliqué-style designs, blouse/dress/saree/kids wear embroidery and embroidery job work.

✨ Maggam & Embellishment

Maggam work, hand embellishment, bead, stone, sequin, pearl/moti, dori, thread, mirror, patch and appliqué-style decorative work, subject to design and material requirements.

🎀 Saree & Finishing

Saree Kuchhulu, tassel making, Fall & Pico, edging, border finishing, lace attachment and saree ironing/finishing.

👧 Kids & Ethnic Wear

Kids embroidery, kids dress stitching and alterations, party/ethnic wear, frock, gown, langa/lehenga-style garments, kurti, salwar/churidar and related fitting work.

🏫 Uniform & Bulk

School, college and company uniform tailoring/alterations, school and business logo embroidery, name embroidery, badges, patches, team/workwear embroidery and bulk orders.
