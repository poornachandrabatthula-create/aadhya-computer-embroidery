Aadhya Final Design Research
============================
The final visual direction was informed by current Indian embroidery/blouse/tailoring sites reviewed in September 2026. Common high-conversion patterns included:
- large editorial hero headline and clear service proposition
- prominent service/category cards
- custom-design/reference-photo enquiry flow
- bridal/marriage positioning
- trust proof (experience, orders, ratings/reviews)
- portfolio/gallery emphasis
- WhatsApp-first contact and mobile sticky actions
- FAQ and local SEO content

Aadhya-specific strengths are prioritized instead of copying competitors: 4+ years experience, 50+ marriage orders, computer embroidery, Maggam works, blouse stitching, kids embroidery, logo embroidery, saree Kuchhulu, Pick & Fall and saree ironing.
